## Hampton research is broader than an admission gate

The recovered program includes typed state, quotient feedback, coupling, representation, reasoning, memory, resource allocation, learning, relationships, narrative continuity, identity and embodiment. Native integration exists for parts of these layers. A source file, imported equation or successful synthetic test does not establish that every layer has an everyday consumer.

The next substantial work is a complete useful-method loop and source-correction workflow, followed by qualified measurement-to-adaptation integration and explicit cross-device transfer. Prediction/world-model consumers, independent external-effect reconciliation and concrete consumers across the nine development channels remain separate tasks. Self-play/distillation training, learned wiki retrieval, JEPA and attention-mask control are research directions, not installed capabilities. The full recovered R3 atlas has not been recovered locally for an equation-by-equation completeness audit.
